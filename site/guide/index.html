Manager · Catalog › Products

A vendor drops off a case with its lab report

Twenty-four new gummies are on the counter and the lab report is in the box. Nothing reaches the menu until its lab record is checked, so start from the paperwork.

  1. Start a draft On Products, press Add with AI, then Start a new draft.
  2. Add the label and the lab report Add photos of the label and the lab report, then press Fill everything. Green Loom reads both and fills in what it finds.
  3. Check the lab record and lot Confirm the lab, the valid-until date and the lot it read. Type the lot if it is missing, then press Next.
  4. Review and publish At Ready to save?, press Save and publish. Press Save as draft instead to finish later.

A product can be published only with a checked, in-date lab record.

Crew · Orders › Board

Noon: the same-day window opens

Orders land as Needs review. Each card on the Board shows one button, the next step for that order. Press it and the card moves along.

  1. Accept the order Press Accept…, read what it says, then press Accept order. Accepting takes the held stock off the on-hand count.
  2. Pack it Press Start packing when you begin, then Mark ready when the bag is sealed.
  3. Hand it to the courier Press Dispatch…, type the courier’s name, then press Dispatch.
  4. Confirm it was delivered When the courier is back, press Confirm delivered…. Tick that you collected payment and checked ID, type who received it, then press Confirm delivered.

Shipped orders ask for a carrier and tracking number at Mark shipped… instead, and delivery is confirmed separately.

Crew · Orders › Board

A pickup customer is at the counter

Their order is Ready. Goods change hands only after the customer’s ID and age are checked.

  1. Open the handoff On the order’s card, press Hand off….
  2. Check ID, then complete Check the customer’s government ID, tick the box that you did and collected payment, then press Complete handoff. Green Loom records that the check happened, never the ID itself.
  3. If the ID doesn’t match Don’t complete the handoff. Press Record exception…, choose ID check failed, say what happened and press Record exception. The order stays open: you can Retry it or Cancel fulfillment… afterwards.

Complete handoff stays locked until the ID box is ticked.

Crew · Orders › Board

The driver is back: nobody was home

The courier couldn’t hand the order over. Record why, so the order shows what happened and what to do next.

  1. Open the exception On the order’s card, press Record exception….
  2. Say what went wrong Choose Customer not home, note what happened, then press Record exception.
  3. Retry or cancel The order shows Needs attention. Press Retry to make it ready for another attempt, or Cancel fulfillment… to send its items back to waiting for a new fulfillment. Stock isn’t changed.

An exception keeps the order open instead of losing it.

Crew · Orders › Messages

A customer asks where their order is

A shopper wrote from their order page. Messages shows threads waiting on you first.

  1. Open the waiting thread Press Messages under Orders. Threads marked Waiting are the ones the customer wrote last. Open one.
  2. Reply Type your answer in Message and press Send.
  3. Check it moved The thread now reads Answered. It shows Waiting again if the customer writes back.

Messages lights up on every admin page while something is waiting.

Crew · Catalog › Stock

The closing count is two short

The shelf has 22 and the system says 24. Fix the system, and say why.

  1. Find the product Open Stock under Catalog and press Adjust on the product’s row.
  2. Enter the change and a reason Type the change in Change: -2 removes two, +5 adds five. Type a Reason, which is required and saved with the change.
  3. Save it Press Save adjustment. On hand updates and the change is added to the product’s History.

Available is calculated, so it can’t be typed. You change what’s on hand, with a reason.

Manager · Store › Pauses & closures

A driver called in sick on Friday

Pause local delivery so shoppers aren’t promised a window you can’t meet. Pickup keeps going.

  1. Open Pauses & closures Under Store, open Pauses & closures and press Pause methods.
  2. Choose what to pause Tick Local delivery. Add a Reason for staff and a Notice shoppers see: say what is paused and when it comes back. Press Pause methods.
  3. Resume Leave Resume time empty to resume by hand: press Resume now when the driver is back. Or set a Resume time and the method reopens on its own.

A pause isn’t a legal closure. Closing the store has its own button, and reopening takes a second approver.
