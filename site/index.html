The rules are changing faster than stores can keep up

70%

70% of surveyed U.S. hemp businesses say they will close or move out of the country under the new federal hemp rules.

Hemp operators in 35 states, surveyed June–July 2026. Whitney Economics, September 2026

Most hemp-THC products are set to lose federal hemp status on December 11, 2026. Georgia already sets its own rules for lab testing, labels and age. Staying open means showing, every day, that each product and each sale meets them.

Why that’s hard today

  • The proof lives outside the store

    Lab reports sit in a folder or an inbox. Nothing stops a product from staying on the menu after its report goes out of date.

  • Age checks never reach the order

    A checkbox online, a glance at the counter, and nothing that ties either one to the order.

  • Every rule change is a manual sweep

    A term you can no longer use, or an order to stop selling, means going through every listing, label and channel by hand.

Green Loom builds those checks into the work. The lab record decides what can be published, the order carries its age check to the counter, and a rule change is one list or one action.

Every sale passes the same checks

Follow one batch from its lab report to the counter. Pick any step to watch it.

  1. Check the lab report: enter the batch’s lab report once and check it.
  2. Publish only what’s tested: a product reaches the menu only with a checked, in-date lab record.
  3. Confirm age at checkout: shoppers confirm they’re 21 or older and are told to bring ID.
  4. Keep the order’s facts together: payment, held stock and the age check travel with the order.
  5. Check ID at the counter: handoff completes only after staff confirm the ID check and payment.

Built from the rule text, not around it

Before designing screens, we read Georgia’s hemp rules and the Department of Agriculture’s guidance at the source, and mapped each requirement to the record it belongs on. We published that work as the Cannabis Product Schema, an open data format with a Georgia hemp profile.

Stop selling in one action. Reopen with two people.

When the law requires the store to stop selling, one action stops ordering on every method. Reopening takes a recorded reason and approval from two different people.

  • Held stock is released and open orders are flagged for review
  • Each approver confirms with their own authenticator code
Starting a legal closure stops ordering. Reopening needs two different people.
