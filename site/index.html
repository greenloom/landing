Where does Georgia grow from here?

We’re building software that makes it easier to open a hemp store, serve customers, and stay on top of the rules.

70%

of surveyed U.S. hemp businesses say they will close or move out of the country under the new federal hemp rules.

Most hemp-THC products are set to lose federal hemp status on December 11, 2026. Stores that stay open will need to rework catalogs, labels and COAs quickly, and Green Loom keeps that work in one place.

From lab result to handoff, one connected record

Green Loom links every step a hemp sale depends on, so a problem at one step stops the sale before it reaches a customer.

A product passes five checks: lab result, listing, stock, order and handoff.

Catalog, orders and customers in one place

The three places a store’s day happens: the product catalog, the order board and the customer record.

An expired lab result blocks the listing, not the product

Each product keeps its identity, packages and copy. When its COA expires, the store listing returns to Draft and says why. Link the new report and publish again.

  • Add with AI reads the label, lab report and receipt before writing copy
  • Expiring COAs are flagged 30 days ahead
  • The price book shows sizes by tier, with profit in every cell
Sample: a listing returns to Draft when its lab result expires.

Every order on one board, from hold to handoff

Orders move through four lanes, On hold, In queue, Packing and Dispatched, and each card shows when its stock hold runs out. Handoff stays locked until payment, items and the ID check are all in place.

  • Pickup, local delivery and shipping on one board
  • Pause one delivery method without closing the store
  • A private order page with status and chat for each customer
Sample: handoff unlocks once payment, items and the ID check are done.

Turn first visits into regulars

Every order belongs to a customer record. Run a members club with versioned rules and loyalty points that build with every purchase.

  • 1 point per $1 of product
  • Member pricing on birthdays and 4/20
  • Club terms re-confirmed every 90 days
Sample: a $48 order adds 48 points to a member's record.
